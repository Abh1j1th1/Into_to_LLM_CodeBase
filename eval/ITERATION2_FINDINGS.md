Key takeaways:
1. **Early unverified answering generalizes across models**: In `c0`, `openai/gpt-oss-120b` (75.0%), `openai/gpt-oss-20b` (65.8%), and `openai/gpt-oss-safeguard-20b` (65.8%) answered after `<= 1` command in the majority of episodes (`early_unverified_answer` was the single largest failure mode). By contrast, `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` had a lower `<= 1`-command rate (22.5%) and instead failed mainly via action loops / TLE (25.0% TLE on OS).
2. **Simple repeat warnings (`c1`) are insufficient**: On `gpt-oss-20b`, `c1` left the `<= 1`-command rate at 60.5% and scored 47.4% vs 52.6% baseline.
3. **Targeted verify gate (`c3`) and memory + verify (`c4`) eliminate premature answers**: Both dropped `<= 1`-command answering to **0.0%** across all tested models, improved DBBench accuracy on `gpt-oss-safeguard-20b` (`c3`: 73.7% with +4/-0 flips; `c4`: 70.6% with +2/-0 flips) and `gpt-oss-120b` (`c4`: 81.2% with +1/-0 flips), with **zero right-to-wrong harm** on DBBench. Trade-off: prompt tokens roughly doubled (`~1.8k–2.1k` -> `~3.8k–7.7k` tokens/episode), and on OS's tight 8-round cap extra verification steps can increase TLE pressure.

## 2. Proposal 1 Results (Trajectory-Aware Bayesian IRT)

Evaluated across 10 observed model/condition configurations on the 40 stratified items + repeated $T=0.7$ (`k=3`) runs on `openai/gpt-oss-safeguard-20b`:

- **Held-out 5-fold CV instance prediction**:
  - `traj_2pl`: **0.8412** accuracy (Brier `0.1234`, Log-Loss `0.3863`)
  - `outcome_2pl`: 0.8339 accuracy (Brier `0.1045`, Log-Loss `0.3301`)
  - `outcome_1pl`: 0.8375 accuracy (Brier `0.1080`, Log-Loss `0.3484`)
- **Calibration-subset full-score recovery (MAE / RMSE vs full set)**:
  - **20% budget (`k=8`)**: `info_traj__traj_2pl` achieved **MAE = 0.0762, RMSE = 0.1008** ($\tau = 0.644, \rho = 0.806$), vs `random__reciprocal_weighted` **MAE = 0.2137, RMSE = 0.2562** ($\tau = 0.450, \rho = 0.558$).
  - **30% budget (`k=12`)**: `info_traj__traj_2pl` achieved **MAE = 0.0650, RMSE = 0.0853** ($\tau = 0.556, \rho = 0.746$), and `info_traj__macro_mean` achieved $\tau = 0.782, \rho = 0.862$, vs `random__reciprocal_weighted` **MAE = 0.1467, RMSE = 0.1866** ($\tau = 0.516, \rho = 0.648$).
- **Leave-one-environment sensitivity (dropping OS)**:
  - `traj_2pl` retained $\tau = 0.6889, \rho = 0.8545$, outperforming `reciprocal_weighted` ($\tau = 0.3680, \rho = 0.5260$).
- **Run-to-run variance at `temperature=0.7` (`k=3`, 15 DBBench items, `gpt-oss-safeguard-20b`)**:
  - Mean score across runs: `0.556 ± 0.038` | **Best-of-3: `0.733`** (+17.7 pp) | Mean per-item variance: `0.0889` (`SD = 0.154`).
